**Pending written confirmation from the original data provider:**

1. Physical units of the workbook field `Aylık Toplam Akım`.
2. Whether records represent monthly mean discharge, monthly integrated volume, or another quantity; exact aggregation convention.
3. Station identifier/period compatibility with the source series.
4. Permission to republish original monthly observations on a publicly accessible GitHub repository (and under what licence).
5. Whether derived row-level series and predictions may be released.
